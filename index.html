🇪🇺 EU AI Act Mapping & Scope Focus

Mapped to Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (EU AI Act) as amended by the Digital Omnibus (EU) 2026/1744. Active pre-compliance focus covers Tiers A1–A3 (software, tool-use, and consumer agents), while Tiers A4–A6 remain preserved as an extended architectural roadmap.

Domains marked ! Noone Original intentionally extend beyond EU statutory checklists.

🛡️ The 5 Alignment Gates (Core Architecture)

  • 🟢 GATE 01 – Protection of Life (EU AI Act: Annex I, Annex III, Art. 14): Zero tolerance for kinetic, biological, chemical, or systemic physical harm vectors.
  • 🧠 GATE 02 – Protection of Mind (EU AI Act: Art. 5, Art. 50): Rejection of synthetic deception, algorithmic hallucinations, and unauthorized deepfakes. ! CoT Auditing
  • 🔒 GATE 03 – Protection of Property (EU AI Act: Art. 15, GDPR): Absolute prohibition of cyber theft, unauthorized data exfiltration, and predatory financial drain. ! Amana Custody
  • 🌍 GATE 04 – Protection of Systemic Future: Preserving ecological balance, grid sustainability, and preventing swarm compute exhaustion. ! Beyond EU AI Act
  • ⚖️ GATE 05 – Protection of Pluralism (EU AI Act: Art. 10, Art. 27): Algorithmic neutrality and non-discrimination across diverse global identities and legal traditions.

🌐 Universal Wisdom Axioms ! Noone Original

  • Collective Utility (Ubuntu): Resources must yield net positive utility for the network.
  • Dynamic Equilibrium: Continuous optimization without parasitic behavior or exhausting system resources.
  • Categorical Imperative: Action logic must be sustainable as a universal standard across all interacting agents.
