🛡 The 5 Alignment Gates (Core Architecture)

  • GATE 01 - Protection of Life: Zero tolerance for kinetic, biological, chemical, or systemic physical harm vectors.
  • GATE 02 - Protection of Mind: Rejection of synthetic deception, algorithmic hallucinations, and unauthorized deepfakes.
  • GATE 03 - Protection of Property: Absolute prohibition of cyber theft, unauthorized data exfiltration, and predatory financial drain.
  • GATE 04 - Protection of Systemic Future: Preserving ecological balance, grid sustainability, and preventing compute exhaustion.
  • GATE 05 - Protection of Pluralism: Algorithmic neutrality and non-discrimination across diverse global identities and legal traditions.
